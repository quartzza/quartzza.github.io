01 · The question

Our main hypothesis is that a higher critic Metascore is associated with higher worldwide box office revenue. A second hypothesis asks whether that relationship differs across Action, Comedy, Drama, and Horror.

05 · Myth vs. Reality

CONVENTIONAL WISDOM
“A higher critic score means a movie will make more money.”
WHAT OUR DATA SHOW
Higher Metascore is positively associated with modeled worldwide gross after controlling for budget, year, and genre. But the relationship is observational — it does not prove that improving a critic score causes revenue to rise.

07 · Limits & robustness

Observational

No randomized treatment. Reverse causation and unmeasured confounding remain possible.

Genre coding

About 143/385 films have multiple target genres. The moderation result is near p=.05 and is sensitive to coding choices.

2025 coverage

2025 is partial. The 2022–2024 sensitivity check gives 22.2% vs 22.9% in the main analysis.

Bottom line: The strongest evidence is a positive adjusted association between Metascore and modeled worldwide gross. The result is useful for decision-making, but should not be presented as a causal effect.
